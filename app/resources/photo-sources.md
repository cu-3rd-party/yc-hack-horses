# Demo photographs

The built-in image generation tool produced the offline fixtures in `app/public/photos`. Each archive diptych was split into two observation photos. The segmentation outlines and trait positions are hand-traced demo annotations.

## pair

One single wide 3:2 documentary field photograph. Two separate wild Przewalski horses, both fully visible side-on with no overlap, one in the left half facing left, the second in right half facing right. They stand on sun-warmed pale steppe grass, distant hazy blue rolling Mongolian hills, open late summer light, natural muted gold/olive. Horses large enough for clear individual markings. Eye-level conservation photographer, honest natural detail, 85mm. No people, captions or graphics.

## saran

A precise vertical diptych contact print made of TWO equally tall wide 3:2 natural documentary photos stacked vertically, edge-to-edge, no margin, no borders, no writing. Photograph the SAME pale golden dun Przewalski mare in both photos, cream muzzle, short erect dark mane. Upper: full body side view, looking LEFT, centered on sunlit grassy steppe under a pale sky. Lower: close three-quarter head and shoulders facing the camera, soft green shrubs. Anatomically accurate field photography for species ID, realistic natural color.

## buran

Vertical photographic diptych of TWO equally tall horizontal 3:2 frames stacked exactly at center edge-to-edge without any margins, lettering or border. A stocky reddish ochre Przewalski stallion, dark short erect mane, pale muzzle and dark legs, same individual in both photos. Upper: full body side view looking right standing on a dusty steppe trail with muted sage grasses. Lower: closer three quarter head turned left, evening light, soft dry grass background. Real wildlife monitoring photography, no people.

## luna

Vertical two-photo contact print, two equally high landscape 3:2 photos stacked vertically, no borders, fonts or text. Same young slender sandy-gray Przewalski mare with dark ears and erect mane in both pictures. Top image shows whole horse grazing in side profile facing left, vivid soft green spring valley. Bottom shows whole animal almost head-on with head lifted, overcast sky, small wildflowers. Naturalistic conservation field photography.

## altai

Vertical diptych with TWO equal-height horizontal 3:2 photographs stacked edge to edge; no gaps or lettering. Same mature dark dun Przewalski horse with thick upright black mane and a warm cream muzzle. Upper image: full body standing left-facing, tawny September grass and a line of silver birches in distant bokeh. Lower image: documentary closeup profile portrait looking right, autumn hills. Authentic wildlife photography.

## single

Photorealistic conservation field photograph, wide 3:2 frame. One pale honey-colored Przewalski mare with a bold upright black mane and cream muzzle, full body in side profile looking left, ears forward, standing in a vast sunlit Central Asian steppe. Horse occupies middle 62% of frame and is placed right of center. Layers of soft sage distant hills, bleached flax grasses and rich September golden light. Editorial wildlife photography with organic film texture. No saddle, humans, text, badges or graphics.
